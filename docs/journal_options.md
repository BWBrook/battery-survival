# Journal options for the battery-survival manuscript

Checked 18 September 2026 against the journals' current publisher pages. This is
a comparative fit note, not a journal decision. Open-access costs are intentionally
not recorded here; confirm current charges, waivers and institutional agreements
after a venue is selected.

| Journal | Current scope and format facts | Fit for this manuscript | OA and practical considerations |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Wildlife Research](https://www.publish.csiro.au/wr) | Covers ecology, management and conservation of wild animals in natural and modified habitats. Its Research Article limit is 6,000 words (up to 80 references); the abstract is up to 350 words with Context, Aims, Methods, Key results, Conclusions and Implications headings. Format-free submission is available, with line numbers and author statements required. | Best current fit. The Tasmanian camera-network reliability and servicing question is applied wildlife monitoring in the journal's geographic and management space. The revised draft is below the Research Article word limit; its abstract would need the required headings. | Hybrid subscription with open-access options. The format-free route lowers immediate style work, while the journal's Australian wildlife readership is a practical advantage. |
| [Wildlife Society Bulletin](https://wildlife.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/23285540) | Open-access journal aimed at wildlife practitioners, integrating science with management, conservation and policy. The official overview explicitly includes management practices, technique evaluation and retrospective programme analyses. A 5,000-word maximum is stated for From the Field, Opinion and Tools and Technology articles; a Research Article limit should be checked in the current author guide before choosing the article type. | Strong alternative if the paper is reframed around a concrete servicing and survey-management decision, with model detail condensed and practical implications foregrounded. The revised management emphasis improves this fit, provided the conditional scenarios are useful to practitioners. | Wiley open-access portfolio; accepted submissions are subject to publication charges unless a waiver applies, and data sharing is expected. Verify the applicable article type and any institutional agreement. |
| [European Journal of Wildlife Research](https://link.springer.com/journal/10344) | Comprehensive wildlife ecology and management worldwide; accepts Research, Review, Brief Report, Comment, Perspective, Methodology and Correspondence. Research papers must present new results. The current instructions specify 13,000 characters and two display items for Brief Reports and Methodology papers, 150–250-word abstracts, 4–6 keywords, Word submission and author declarations. | Credible international option for the empirical survival and monitoring-method contribution, especially if the manuscript is presented as a broadly transferable wildlife-monitoring reliability study. It is less specifically aligned with Australian applied readership than Wildlife Research. | Hybrid Springer journal with open-access funding links. The continuous article-publishing model uses article citation IDs rather than sequential issue pages. Verify Springer’s current author-type requirements and OA eligibility before submission. |

Provisional fit ranking: Wildlife Research first, Wildlife Society Bulletin second if
the management framing is strengthened, and European Journal of Wildlife Research
third for a more internationally framed methods contribution. Barry retains the
venue decision; no submission or manuscript-source change follows from this note.

Official author guidance: [Wildlife Research](https://connectsci.au/wr/pages/wr-authors),
[European Journal of Wildlife Research](https://link.springer.com/journal/10344/submission-guidelines),
and [Wildlife Society Bulletin overview](https://wildlife.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/23285540).
