# Field-methods questions for the author editing pass

19 September 2026. These facts need author confirmation before co-author circulation. Unknowns are not filled from model results or assumed from the old outline. Answers can be concise; distinctions between deployment subsets matter more than apparent completeness.

1. **Dates and sampling frame.** What years do the records cover, which projects/sites supplied them, and how were sites and service records selected? Do hardware or allocation practices differ by year or region?
2. **Camera hardware and settings.** Did all analytical records use Cuddeback 1279 cameras, eight AA cells and interchangeable flash units, as the original outline suggests? What trigger, burst, delay and recording settings applied, and were they consistent? What variation is documented?
3. **Battery sets and allocation.** Did every interval begin with a complete fresh set? Were brands, chemistry, batches or purchase dates tracked, and were brands allocated by availability, site, season, camera or experiment? Could sets be partly replaced or reused?
4. **Identifier continuity.** Does a camera identifier follow a physical camera throughout the dataset, including repairs, replacements and moves? How were relocations or reused identifiers recorded? The analysis models the recorded identifier and does not equate it with a site.
5. **Visits and censoring.** Were visits planned on a fixed schedule, opportunistic, triggered by suspected failure or related to image workload? Did visits or battery replacement depend on brand, site access, season or camera state? How was season assigned to intervals spanning seasons?
6. **Endpoint classification.** How were a dead battery and an internal power error distinguished? What evidence excluded card, camera, clock, theft or other failures, and how were ambiguous cases coded? Is the last-image timestamp consistently the final retrievable valid image?

Service-date upper bounds for cameras found inactive are unavailable, as already confirmed. This revision does not request their recovery. SW differences in camera age and the drier/windier coastal environment remain hypotheses, not established explanations. The paper should say what is documented and retain uncertainty where records cannot resolve these questions.
