# Servicing interpretation and Makedoc backbone

## Scope and decision

Barry authorised settling the servicing-cost interpretation and adding the
publication backbone after the initial orientation. Work began from clean main
at `d2fb2a1`; the Mac mini master checkout had the same commit and unchanged
publication sources. Changes in this tranche are local to the WSL checkout;
the Mac mini master repository was not edited or updated.

The fixed-cycle cost objective is non-increasing with service interval and
cannot identify an interior optimum balancing cost and downtime. The active
optima summary/table/figure have been removed, while the numerical cost and
downtime grid, profiles, costs, model fits, interpolation and raw data are
unchanged. See `../servicing_cost_interpretation.md` for the decision and proof.
No tolerance, penalty for lost observations, or new field schedule was chosen.

## Numerical evidence used

The backbone uses saved full-data outputs recovered read-only from
`dev-box-ts:/Users/bwbrook/papers/battery-survival/outputs/`. These files are
dated 16 June 2026; they were not newly generated by the September source
checkout. Their headline values agree with the September 5 handover. The
backbone does not use the March development-profile baseline as full-data
evidence.

The local recovered files are under ignored `outputs/`:

- `tables/partition_summary.csv`: cohort sizes and event/censoring counts.
- `tables/km_median_survival_by_brand.csv`: five reported training medians.

  estimates, camera-cluster robust intervals and global PH diagnostic.

## Manuscript and build

`manuscript/text/main.md` now contains a substantive backbone: working abstract,
ecological motivation, methods, numerical results, the revised servicing
interpretation, discussion and conclusions. A cohort table and five captioned
figures support the text. Field-method and cost-basis questions are visible in
the draft. Author contributions, acknowledgements, authorship, full literature
integration and venue decisions remain open.

`settings/build.yaml` replaces the inactive template and uses the current
external-path Makedoc contract. It selects a private backbone profile, a neutral
author-date CSL, and five DOI-verified starter references. Unconfirmed author
metadata is omitted. The bibliography must later be reconciled with the
authors' canonical library; no journal was selected. Output is PDF and DOCX
under `outputs/publication/`.

## Verification

- All R tests passed in an isolated temporary check on the Mac mini: 109
  expectations, zero failures, errors or skips. The check used R 4.6.1 and
  available user libraries with `Rscript --vanilla`; it was not a fresh locked
  renv restoration or a production pipeline rerun.
- `targets::tar_manifest()` parsed the revised 76-target DAG; retired optima
  targets were absent. Source review verified the retained numerical grid
  construction is unchanged.
- The economics tests also passed locally on WSL and check non-increasing
  cost, non-decreasing downtime and downtime fraction, and the invariance of
  interval ranking to service-cost scaling.
- Makedoc config validation and `make check` passed, including manuscript lint,
  reference audit (five cited keys, zero missing or unused), and PDF/DOCX
  generation. These are document checks, not a submission-readiness verdict.
- Final native PDF visual review covered all 14 A4 pages. The DOCX was rendered
  with LibreOffice and independently inspected across all 16 pages; its Letter
  geometry is the existing Makedoc Word default. Both proofs have intact text,
  equations, references, one cohort table and five figures. A page break before
  Methods keeps the short table together. The five original figure files were
  preserved, so label refinement remains later manuscript polish.
- An independent scientific review checked the calculus, removal of the optima
  products, and every numerical manuscript claim against the recovered tables.
  It requested consistent weak-monotonic wording and this provenance note;
  both were incorporated. It found no need for additional modelling for the
  backbone.
- The prose scanner was reviewed manually. Its high score was dominated by
  required Markdown headings, table syntax and the statistical term 'robust';
  these were retained. One focused prose/layout repair pass was used.

The full production analysis and Quarto report were not rerun. No Bayesian
sampling, new empirical analysis, dependency installation, commit, push or
publication was performed. Any later substantial analysis should use
deep-server and a persistent tmux session under the standing compute policy.
