## Scope and synthesis

Barry authorised manuscript integration after reviewing the completed September
17 sensitivity analysis. This tranche revises the publication source and its
build/display workflow. It does not refit models, change observations or
exclusions, or replace the canonical targets outputs or archived sensitivity
results. The revised paper remains a private working draft.

The abstract, methods, results and discussion now distinguish the original
common-scale, no-region Weibull analysis from the expanded 12-model comparison.

marginalisation, within-model uncertainty and descriptive support flags. It
retains the minimum-confirmed-runtime convention and treats service dates as
unavailable, rather than leaving reconstruction as a prerequisite.

camera-days. Their optima and implications are not conflated. The 2.6-day
camera-adjustment result is restricted to the evaluated support-filtered
comparisons; the family-sensitive infrared and thin-tail examples remain
visible. Failed SW transfer is neither explained by speculation nor presented
as repaired by the new fits.

### Final posterior-tail qualification and delivery checks

A last methodological check found that exact unrestricted posterior predictive
means can diverge under the unbounded scale priors, even when all retained draws
have finite conditional means. This applies immediately to loglogistic scales
at or above one, and potentially to Weibull through rapid gamma-function growth
in its conditional mean. The numerical no-finite classifications are therefore
explicitly defined as properties of the 1,000-draw empirical predictive mixture.
The abstract says 'no supported finite solution'. Supplementary Methods explain
why a finite simulation cannot resolve an unrestricted optimum in these remote
tails. Finite-time costs and the six consensus numerical preferences are
unchanged; no new fits or prior truncation were introduced. This qualification
was independently checked and posted to Fable as message219 in thread42.

### Changes and results

- New `R/management_break_even.R`, `scripts/summarise_break_even.R` and
  `tests/testthat/test-management-break-even.R`; `just break-even` is the
  entrypoint. The script reuses 1,000 paired draws per saved camera model and
  the existing 30-node integration over new-camera effects. It calculates the
  threshold equating posterior expected losses, with paired parameter-draw
  uncertainty. Nonpositive downtime differences retain an infinite crossing
  value; no such draws occurred in these 16 comparisons, which is not proof
  of exact zero posterior probability.
- At 120 days the expected-loss thresholds span 0.95–1.54 illustrative cost
  units per lost camera-day, equivalent to about 2–3% of the Energizer set
  price per lost day. At 90 days they span 3.33–9.02. Family differences reach
  24.8% at 90 days, but are below 5% at 120. Uncertainty is wider at 90 days:
  Ansmann/Weibull is 7.25 [4.36,15.11], versus 1.54 [1.12,2.32] at 120.

  Energizer reference. Procell's 120-day 19 intervals/16 cameras is flagged
