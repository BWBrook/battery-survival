Authorised by Barry on 19 September 2026 after the independent Astra/max review.

supersedes the marginal-mode profile rule, not the archived fitted models.

## Prediction target

Continue comparing illustrative operating scenarios within each observed
battery/flash group. Select its most frequent **joint** region/season cell;
resolve ties by lexical region then season. Within that cell, use the observed
pair of log-one-plus flash/daylight rates nearest the two cell medians by
squared Euclidean distance. Both rates share the same transformed units. Break
ties lexically by flash then daylight rate. This gives an observed categorical
combination and observed workload pair, without using survival outcomes to
select a scenario. Publish the selected values, cell sizes and workload ranges.
This is not standardisation to a common deployment population or a causal
brand comparison. It is a replacement of the prior typical-profile construction
under an explicit, uniform rule, not a post-hoc choice to retain its headline.
