![Expected lost observation time as a function of the revisit interval for the five white-flash scenarios. (a) Expected lost camera-days per service cycle; (b) additional expected lost camera-days relative to the Energizer scenario. Solid lines and shading show posterior means and 95% intervals from the Weibull camera-effect model; dashed lines show the loglogistic model. Each curve stops at the maximum follow-up observed in its joint brand/flash/region/season cell (open circle). Faded segments have fewer than 20 intervals or 10 cameras at risk; maximum follow-up alone does not establish adequate support. Dotted lines mark 90 and 120 days. Each scenario combines a battery with an observed region, season and retrospective workload (CT = Central, SE = South East, WT = West; Table S5), so curves compare observed deployments rather than effects of switching batteries at one site. Lost time is modelled under the last-image-as-failure convention, not measured inactivity.](../../outputs/management/2026-10-18/lost_day_curves/lost_day_curves.png){#fig-lost-day-curves width=100%}
