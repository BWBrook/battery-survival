![Recorded operational lifetime by battery brand in the TAS-CAM training data. Kaplan–Meier curves treat cameras still operating at service as right-censored and use the last-image time for cameras found non-functioning. Grey segments have fewer than ten deployment intervals at risk, and the table beneath the plot gives pooled numbers at risk. The curves describe the observed mix of deployments and are not adjusted battery-treatment effects.](../../outputs/publication/figures/km_by_brand.png){#fig-km-brand width=95%}

![Covariate associations in the region-stratified Cox model, shown as hazard ratios with 95% camera-cluster robust confidence intervals on a logarithmic axis. Values above one indicate a higher failure rate than the reference category: Ansmann for brand, infrared for flash and autumn for season. Workload effects are per unit of log-one-plus realised flash or daylight images per recorded day. Because the global proportional-hazards test was non-null, the ratios summarise associations over follow-up rather than constant effects.](../../outputs/publication/figures/cox_hazard_ratios.png){#fig-cox-hazard-ratios width=95%}

![Training calibration of the original common-scale, no-region Weibull model and of expanded Weibull and loglogistic models with brand-specific scales and region effects. Curves are predictions averaged over each brand's observed covariates, not predictions at an average covariate vector. Points and bars are Kaplan–Meier estimates with pointwise 95% intervals, which do not adjust for recurring cameras; grey points have fewer than ten deployment intervals at risk, and the counts in each panel title correspond to the seven plotted horizons. These frequentist fits treat deployment rows as independent. They are fitted-data diagnostics, and their late portions rest on sparse follow-up.](../../outputs/publication/figures/aft_calibration.png){#fig-aft-calibration width=95%}

![Sensitivity of conditional servicing intervals to lifetime family and camera dependence. Points and bars are posterior medians and 95% credible intervals at assumed ratios of cycle cost to lost-day value; because battery prices differ, equal ratios are not equal-price comparisons (see Table 2). Grey shading exceeds the follow-up of the matching brand/flash/region/season cell, and open points flag a greater than 50% probability of thin support in that cell among finite optima. Predictions integrate over new-camera effects. Medians above 350 days are omitted and intervals clipped. These are model diagnostics, not recommended schedules; Supplementary Section S4 gives definitions and support limits.](../../outputs/publication/figures/camera_decisions.png){#fig-camera-decisions width=95%}
